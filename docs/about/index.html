About

Research across the computing stack.

I am a computer science student and undergraduate researcher at the University of California, Riverside. My work spans microarchitecture simulation, secure processing-in-memory systems, computational genomics, embedded hardware, and test automation.

Research

Three labs at UC Riverside.

I move between architecture, security, and genomics with the same approach: make the system measurable, build the tooling, and validate the result.

01Brisk Lab

June 2026 – Present

Researched and documented an Intel Simics and MacSim integration pipeline using C/C++, Intel XED, and Python for multicore instruction tracing, binary trace generation, and sampled warm-up and detail execution.

Explore adaptive cache warming

02Sadredini Lab

April 2026 – Present

Conducted research on secure processing-in-memory systems, studying MPC techniques such as arithmetic secret sharing and garbled circuits for secure computation on untrusted hardware.

Explore secure processing-in-memory

03Lonardi Lab

June 2025 – Present

Conduct research on computational genomics under the supervision of Dr. Stefano Lonardi, focusing on genome assembly optimization and k-mer-based read selection methods.

Explore MTP Lite research
